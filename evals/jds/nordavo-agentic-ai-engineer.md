# Agentic AI Engineer (Claude Code, Cursor)
Nordavo · Bangalore, Indiranagar office · Full time · Medior

## About Nordavo
Nordavo connects Dutch tech companies with engineers in Bangalore. Our clients range from SaaS companies building their own product to agencies building AI systems for their customers. You work from our office in Indiranagar, on our payroll, as a full member of the client's team. We handle HR and everything around it.

## The role
Most Dutch tech teams don't write their code by hand anymore. They build with Claude Code, Cursor and Codex, and they need engineers who can make those agents do real work. Not demos. Systems that keep running when nobody is watching. Depending on the client, that means product features, internal tools or automations behind the scenes.

You won't get a ticket with every step written out. You get a problem, some context and a lead who expects you to figure out the rest and come back with a plan.

## What you'll do
You own the agent and everything around it, from the first setup to the review before it goes live.
- Build agents, workflows, features and internal tools with Claude Code, Cursor and other agent harnesses
- Design the setup around each agent: tools, context, permissions, memory and checks
- Work on the layer underneath: servers, databases, APIs, integrations, RAG and vector databases
- Set up evals and logging, and find out why an agent went wrong
- Review what the agent wrote before it goes live. You own the result, not the AI
- Join the team's stand-ups and keep your lead updated without being asked

## Skills we look for
We look for people who already work this way every day, not people who want to learn it.
- You build with Claude Code, Cursor, Codex or a similar agent every day, and you can show what you built with it
- You know how to extend your harness: MCP servers, subagents, hooks, skills, rules and custom commands. You've set these up yourself, not just read about them
- You fix agent problems with context, tools and feedback loops, not only with a better prompt
- You understand what the agent produces. You can read the code, spot when it's wrong and explain every line if someone asks
- Solid basics: Git, pull requests, APIs, databases and debugging
- You think about security. You know where secrets go, what permissions an agent should not have and what happens if it fails halfway
- You keep an eye on cost and speed. A workflow that burns tokens for no reason is a bad workflow
- 2 to 5 years of experience as a developer or engineer

## How you work
- You ask questions until a task is clear, and you say so when you disagree
- You flag problems early and give honest estimates
- You write short, clear updates without being asked
- You take ownership of what you build, also when it breaks

## Nice to have
- n8n, Supabase, Next.js or similar
- Experience working with European or US teams
- A public repo, blog or video about how you work with agents
